Better Translator support

For help or to report a problem, write to kilts-truer-2l@icloud.com. Say which iPhone or Mac you use, which version of iOS or macOS it runs, and what you saw.

Getting started

  1. Allow microphone access when asked.
  2. Download the speech model (about 480 MB) and Apple's Portuguese (Brazil) to English language pack. Use Wi-Fi if you can.
  3. Tap Start and hold the device near the people speaking.

Start is available as soon as the microphone, the speech model and the Portuguese to English pack are ready. Speaker labels, the English to Portuguese pack and Apple Intelligence are optional.

Common questions

Setup shows "Follow the system prompt to download the language pack" and nothing happens. Tap Download again to ask Apple to show its prompt. If it still does not appear, leave the app and come back, which re-checks the pack. Apple's prompt needs a connection; Wi-Fi is best.

Captions say Apple Intelligence is unavailable. The app works without it. Sentences that refer back to earlier ones keep their first translation instead of being adjusted for context. Turn on Apple Intelligence in Settings if your iPhone supports it.

The app stopped when I opened another app. Listening continues in other apps through the floating caption window (Picture in Picture) and the Lock Screen. A phone call or Siri pauses the session, and closing the floating window or dismissing the Lock Screen caption stops it.

A word was misheard. The Portuguese line shows exactly what the recognizer heard. It sometimes mishears a word, and the app does not rewrite it, because that would invent meaning.

A sentence that starts in English and switches to Portuguese shows only the Portuguese. This is a known limit of the speech recognizer.

Where is my transcript? On the Mac, nothing is saved unless you export it. On the iPhone, the current session is kept on the phone only, so the app can offer it back; Clear or Discard deletes it.

How do I remove everything? Delete the app. Its models and settings go with it.
