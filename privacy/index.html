Better Translator privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-09

Better Translator turns Brazilian Portuguese speech near your device into English captions. It is built so that what you say and read stays on your device.

What the app collects

Nothing. Better Translator has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it sends no audio, text or usage data to the developer or to anyone else.

What happens to your speech and captions

  • Audio from the microphone is processed on your device to recognise speech and is not stored.
  • Text of what was heard and its translation is processed on your device. On the Mac it is kept only while the window is open unless you export it. On the iPhone, the current session's finished sentences are kept in one file on the phone so the app can offer them back if iOS closes it. That file is encrypted with iOS data protection (closed to other apps and to anyone without your passcode while the phone is locked), is not included in iCloud or computer backups, and is deleted when you tap Clear or Discard.
  • Settings and vocabulary you enter are stored on your device.
  • Diagnostic logs stay on your device and do not include the text of sentences.
  • Exporting happens only when you choose Copy, Save or Share; what you share leaves the app because you sent it.

Downloads

During setup the app downloads, once:

  • speech-recognition and speaker-label models, from Hugging Face (huggingface.co); and
  • Apple's language packs, from Apple.

These requests download files. They do not send your audio, your text or any identifier of you. Hugging Face and Apple see the connection like any other website visit, under their own policies.

Apple services on your device

Translation and, where your device has them, Apple Intelligence features run on your device through Apple's frameworks. Apple's own privacy terms apply to those frameworks.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date appears at the top, and the app will not begin collecting data without saying so here first.
